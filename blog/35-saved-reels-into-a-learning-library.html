Saved Reels into a learning library

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Short-form video gets dismissed as noise, but look at what people actually save: cooking techniques, Excel shortcuts, negotiation one-liners, gym form fixes. The saves are sincere. The problem is format — a feed built for endless watching is the worst possible library.

Treat each save as a claim

A sixty-second video usually contains exactly one claim worth keeping. Write it as a sentence: "dutch ovens need a cold oven start," "the salary question has a two-step answer." One save, one sentence. If you can't write the sentence, the save was a vibe, not knowledge.

The library that replaces the feed

Once your saves become sentences, they can be grouped, searched, and reviewed. ORANO does the transcription: save a Reel, get the claim extracted with the timestamp, and the ones worth memorizing become flashcards in your review queue. The feed becomes an index of things you actually know.

The payoff

You stop re-saving the same content every six months because the knowledge already lives on your side of the app. And the rare great creator becomes easy to study deliberately instead of accidentally.
