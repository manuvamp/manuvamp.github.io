Save with a trigger condition

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Most saved videos never get watched because there is no moment when watching them is the obvious next action. The content is real, the intent is real — what's missing is a trigger: the specific future situation in which this video becomes the most relevant thing you own.

The format

One line at save time: "when , watch

What the condition does downstream

Trigger-tagged saves change the nature of the library. Search becomes scenario-based ("what do I own about fundraising?"). Conversion becomes targeted (only triggered videos get full summaries and cards). And the queue stops growing, because untriggered saves have nothing protecting them from the delete key. ORANO (oranoai.com) keeps the trigger with the summary, the tasks, and the cards, so the right material resurfaces when the condition fires.

The payoff

You stop collecting content for a future self who doesn't exist, and start storing answers for situations that actually will.
