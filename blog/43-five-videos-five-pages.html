Five videos, five pages, one rule

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

If you save more than five videos a week and produce fewer than five pages of notes, your watching is consumption wearing the costume of study. The constraint that fixes it is embarrassingly small: every saved video earns exactly one page.

What goes on the page

Three sections, nothing else. The claim — what the video argues, in one sentence with a timestamp. The takeaways — up to five bullet points with the evidence offered. The follow-ups — any action the video implies, written as a task with a verb. A page that needs more than this is really two pages, or zero.

Generating the pages

Hand-writing five pages a week is a habit most people cannot sustain, which is why automation belongs here and not in the watching itself. Paste the link into ORANO at save time and the page exists before you would have gotten around to it — summary with timestamps, extracted tasks, flashcards for the durable claims. Your weekly job shrinks to reading five pages and keeping three tasks.

The rule behind the rule

The page is not the goal; it is the receipt that proves retrieval happened. Watch anything you like unrecorded — just stop calling it learning.
