Two devices, one library

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Watch a video on your phone at 11pm, need its summary on your laptop at 9am. That single handoff is where most video-learning systems break: the note lives where the video was watched, or worse, the intention lives only in your head.

Capture on the phone, use on the desktop

Saving should be a phone action — you encounter videos there. Processing and reviewing should be a desktop action — reading and organizing want a keyboard and screen. Any system that requires the same device for both halves will die the week your habits shift. ORANO (oranoai.com) syncs the library across iOS, Android, and web so the save happens wherever you are and the brief is waiting on whatever you open next.

The offline commute layer

The third device is none: the train. Briefs and flashcards synced for offline review turn dead transit time into the retention pass of the learning loop. This is the least glamorous feature in any app and the one that actually gets used daily.

The payoff

One library, three surfaces, zero copy-paste. The videos live on platforms; the knowledge lives with you.
