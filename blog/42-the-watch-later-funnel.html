The Watch Later funnel, redesigned

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Anyone who runs growth will recognize the shape of a Watch Later queue: a wide top of capture, a pinch of qualification, and almost no conversion. Nothing that enters the queue ever exits as knowledge. Applying funnel discipline to the queue fixes it in a week.

Capture with a tag, not a thumb

The default save is one tap and carries no intent. Add a second step: when you save, tag it "why" — learn, reference, or fun. The tag is one second of effort that creates three clean buckets instead of one polluted stream.

Qualify on a schedule

Once a week, the "learn" bucket gets triaged. The test is simple: does this video contain a claim worth keeping or an action worth doing? If yes, it converts to notes and tasks. If no, it was entertainment — archive it without ceremony. ORANO automates this stage: the conversion to summary, tasks, and flashcards happens on save, so weekly triage is a review, not a project.

Convert, then measure

The funnel metric that matters is conversion rate — the share of saves that became notes, tasks, or cards. Mine went from roughly 5% to 60% after adding the intent tag and weekly triage. The queue got smaller and more useful at the same time, which is the whole trick.
