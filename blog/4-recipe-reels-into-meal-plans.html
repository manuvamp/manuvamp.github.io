Recipe Reels into meal plans: the cooking workflow

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Nobody cooks from a bookmark folder. Recipe Reels are saved by the thousand and cooked by the dozen, because a 45-second video with the recipe in the caption is not a plan — it is content.

From save to shopping list

Capture at the moment of hunger. Share the Reel into ORANO. It pulls the transcript and caption, extracts the ingredients and steps, and files the recipe with a time estimate and difficulty.

Plan three meals, not thirty. Open your recipe projects, pick three for the week, and let the task lists do their job: each recipe is an ordered checklist — marinate tonight, chop tomorrow, cook in 20 minutes.

Combine into one grocery run. Three structured recipes mean three ingredient lists that merge into one trip. The folder of dreams becomes a checklist you can execute.

Keep the winners. After cooking, the recipe stays in your library with everything you learned. Search "that chili thing from March" and it comes back — grounded in your own saved context.

This is the same save-to-action loop the app uses for every content type: iOS, Android.
