Podcast episodes into a weekly briefing

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Podcasts are the most disposable knowledge format we consume daily. An hour of insight, heard at 1.5x while doing dishes, is gone by Thursday. The fix is not listening less — it is converting what you hear into one reusable page.

The weekly brief, not the daily note

Don't take notes per episode; that's a chore that dies in a week. Instead, drop each episode into ORANO as you queue it. The episode arrives as a summary with grounded key points and quotable lines. Friday morning, query your week: "what did the three episodes I saved say about hiring?" The briefing writes itself from your own library, with timestamps back to the audio.

From brief to action

A briefing is only useful if it changes something. Each week, pull one action item out of the digest — a book to read, an experiment to run, a person to email. One per week compounds into twenty-six deliberate moves a year, all traceable to a moment in an episode you can replay in fifteen seconds.

The payoff

You keep the listening habit you enjoy and gain a searchable record of everything it taught you. The podcast feed becomes a research assistant instead of background noise.
