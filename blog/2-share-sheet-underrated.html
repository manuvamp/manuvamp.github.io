The share sheet is the most underrated productivity feature on iOS

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Apple's share sheet is fifteen years old and most people use it to send memes. That is a waste of the most powerful routing system on the phone: any app can hand any content to any other app, in one tap, from anywhere.

Share destinations beat bookmarks

When you share a Reel or a YouTube video into a capture tool instead of saving it inside the source app, three things change. The content escapes the platform's walled garden. It can be processed — transcribed, summarized, structured. And it can be combined with the rest of your library instead of living in ten separate apps.

That is exactly how ORANO is built: an iOS Share Extension and an Android share target sit one tap away from every Reel, Short, article, repo, tweet and PDF on your phone. Share it in, and the app fetches and understands the source — transcript, captions, visual context, metadata — and files it as a structured project.

Designing a personal routing table

Once your share sheet sends everything to one place, you can build rules on top: actionable content becomes an ordered task list, reference content becomes searchable takeaways, and everything becomes context your own AI agent can read through a personal MCP server.

The share sheet was never the problem. The destinations were.
