Travel vlogs into itineraries: planning the trip you keep saving

Founder notes · Manu Parasuraman

Saving travel videos is the most pleasant form of procrastination ever invented. Every "3 days in Lisbon" vlog feels like progress toward the trip. But a folder of thirty Lisbon vlogs is not an itinerary — it is thirty conflicting recommendations with no order, no costs, and no bookings.

What extraction turns a vlog into

A good travel video contains a usable itinerary in pieces: the neighborhoods, the restaurants with the dish to order, the timing tricks (go early, skip the line), the costs mentioned in passing, and the mistakes to avoid. Extraction assembles them into a day-by-day plan: ordered stops that make geographic sense, an estimated budget per day, and a booking checklist (the things that actually sell out).

Comparing saves instead of hoarding them

The second superpower is comparison. When each saved vlog becomes a structured project — costs extracted, highlights listed — choosing between "the food tour version of Lisbon" and "the architecture version" is a two-minute read of two summaries, not a three-hour rewatch marathon. ORANO does the extraction on every share, keeps the library searchable, and turns the winner into an actionable plan with an ordered task list.
